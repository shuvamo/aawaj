What is Aawaj?

Aawaj means “voice” in Nepali and Hindi. It’s a free text to speech tool that turns your script into natural-sounding audio.

How it works

The voice model downloads once (about 80 MB) and is then cached by your browser. After that, speech is generated on your own device, so your text is never uploaded.

Can I use the audio commercially?

The voices come from the open-source Kokoro model (Apache 2.0 license). You can use the audio in videos, podcasts, and apps.

Why is the first run slow?

The first run downloads the model. Later runs start in seconds. A desktop browser with WebGPU (recent Chrome or Edge) is fastest.
